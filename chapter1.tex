\section{Research Methodology}
The predominant Goal of this project is to determine a robust method for estimating Respiratory Rate from ECG Signal. The approach of this paper is to first determine 
a robust algorithm for identify the RR peaks of the ECG signal, then using spectrum associated methods (RPA and RSA) to estimate the Respiratory rate over time.
Our Methodology will go as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
        \item Investigate ECG signals, identify datasets with distinct varying behaviour to test the Robustness of our algorithms.
        \item Develop, and refine a algorithm to robustly identify RR peaks.
        \item Utilising the algorithm(s) from 2. Evaluate and test different methods for estimating Respiratory Rate, via RR peaks. 
\end{enumerate}
